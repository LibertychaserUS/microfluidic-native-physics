# h_A1 artefact copies

Byte copies of the manifest/summary files of the A1 validation run. Per-row files (development_labeled.csv, sealed_features.csv, sealed_labels.csv, point_predictions.csv, interval_rows.csv, sealed_prediction_bundle.csv, sealed_scored_rows.csv) stay in the workspace: they contain third-party data (ESI of Hormozinezhad et al. 2025, CC BY 3.0) and are not redistributed here; their SHA-256 digests are in the manifests.
